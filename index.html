What is Real Bat Cricket?

Real Bat Cricket is a cricket game where your smartphone works as a virtual cricket bat using motion controls.

How does motion batting work?

Your phone's motion sensors detect your movement and use it to control the cricket shot.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes, online multiplayer is planned for Real Bat Cricket.

Do I need a special cricket bat?

No. Your smartphone acts as the virtual cricket bat.
